# **Converting decimals to fractions**

- $65.85$ actually means:

```math
\begin{aligned}
(6 \times 10) + (5 \times 1) + \left(8 \times \frac{1}{10}\right) + \left(5 \times \frac{1}{100}\right)
&= 60 + 5 + \frac{8}{10} + \frac{5}{100}\\
&= 65 + \frac{4}{5} + \frac{1}{20}\\
&= \frac{65}{1} + \frac{4}{5} + \frac{1}{20}\\
&= \frac{1300}{20} + \frac{16}{20} + \frac{1}{20}\\
&= \frac{1317}{20}\\
65.85 &= \boxed{65\frac{17}{20}}
\end{aligned}
```
